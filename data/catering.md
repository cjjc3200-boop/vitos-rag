# Vito's Sandwich Shop — Catering

We cater lunches, parties, and events across the Tampa Bay area.

## Catering packages
- **Sandwich Platter** — a mix of our cold sandwiches cut in halves, serves 10–12 people. $95.
- **Hot Sub Tray** — your choice of up to three hot sandwiches, cut and trayed, serves 10–12 people. $130.
- **The Full Spread** — sandwich platter, hot sub tray, two sides, and a dessert tray, serves 20–25 people. $275.

## Lead time
Catering orders require at least **48 hours' notice**. For orders over 50 people, please give us **one week's notice**.

## Delivery for catering
Catering delivery is available within 15 miles of the shop for a flat $25 fee. Set-up (trays arranged, plates and napkins provided) is included for the Full Spread.

## Dietary needs
We can accommodate vegetarian catering. Please tell us about any allergies or dietary needs when you book, and we will label the trays accordingly.

## Booking
Call (813) 555-0142 or email catering@vitossandwiches.com to book catering. A 50% deposit secures the date; the balance is due on delivery.
