# Vito's Sandwich Shop — Allergens & Dietary Information

Please tell the counter staff about any allergy before ordering. We prepare food in a shared kitchen, so we cannot guarantee zero cross-contact.

## Common allergens
- **Gluten** — present in all standard rolls, breaded items (eggplant parm, chicken cutlet), and pasta. Gluten-free rolls are available for +$2.
- **Dairy** — present in provolone, mozzarella, American cheese, Swiss, and our pesto (contains parmesan).
- **Nuts** — we do not use peanuts or tree nuts in our recipes, but our bread supplier handles nuts in their facility.
- **Shellfish** — no shellfish items are prepared on site.
- **Egg** — present in mayonnaise, breaded cutlets, and meatballs.

## Vegetarian
Eggplant Parm, Veggie, and the Caprese are vegetarian. Broccoli rabe and macaroni salad are vegetarian sides.

## Vegan
We do not currently offer vegan cheese or vegan rolls. The Veggie sandwich can be made vegan by removing mozzarella and pesto (olive oil and vinegar instead).

## Halal
We do not serve halal-certified meat at this time.

## Full ingredient lists
Full ingredient lists for any item are available at the counter on request.
