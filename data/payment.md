# Vito's Sandwich Shop — Payment & Policies

## Accepted payment
- Cash
- All major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover)
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Vito's gift cards

We do not accept checks.

## Tipping
Tips are appreciated but never required. There is a tip line on the card reader and a tip jar at the counter.

## Refunds
If you are unhappy with an item, tell us — we will remake it or refund it on the spot. Refunds to card are processed within 3–5 business days.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are available in any amount from $10 up, in store or online. They never expire and can be used for both walk-in and catering orders.

## Loyalty
The Vito's Punch Card: buy 9 sandwiches, get the 10th free. One punch per sandwich, no cash value, not valid on catering or platters.

## Receipts
Itemized receipts are available on request. For catering and business orders, we can email a receipt for expense reporting.
