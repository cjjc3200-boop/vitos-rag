# Vito's Sandwich Shop — Ordering & Delivery

## Ways to order
- **In person** — walk up to the counter. Most sandwiches are ready in 8–12 minutes during normal hours.
- **Phone** — call (813) 555-0142. We recommend calling ahead for orders of 5 or more sandwiches.
- **Online** — order through our website or the link in our Instagram bio for pickup.
- **Delivery** — available within a 3-mile radius through the major delivery apps. Delivery fees and times are set by the app, not by us.

## Order-ahead
Order-ahead by phone or online is available during all open hours. Give your name and a pickup time; we hold orders for 20 minutes past the scheduled pickup time.

## Cancellations & changes
Call us as soon as possible to change or cancel an order. Once a hot sandwich has been started, we may not be able to cancel it.

## Large orders
For orders of 10 or more sandwiches, please give us at least 24 hours' notice.

## Lost or wrong orders
If something is wrong with your order, tell the counter staff right away or call us the same day and we will make it right.
