# Vito's Sandwich Shop — Hours

Vito's Sandwich Shop is open six days a week and closed on Sundays.

- Monday to Friday: 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM
- Saturday: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed

We are closed on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and the 4th of July. On Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve we close early at 4:00 PM.

The kitchen stops taking hot sandwich orders 30 minutes before closing. The deli counter (cold cuts by the pound) stays open until closing time.

Holiday hours are posted on the front door and on our Instagram the week before each holiday.
